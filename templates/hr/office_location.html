What this changes
Clocking with a location
Inside the radius, it just works. Outside it but within the phone's own margin of error, it is recorded and flagged on the roll-call. Beyond both, it is refused — the phone is confident, and confident that it is somewhere else.
Clocking with no location
Allowed only with a scanned attendance code. Without that, turning location off would simply be how somebody clocks in from home.
A supervisor recording for somebody
Never judged. They are at a desk entering an event for a person who was on a site, so measuring the supervisor's phone would refuse the very thing that exemption exists for.
The attendance codes
Unchanged, and carrying no coordinates. A code proves somebody had it in front of them; this proves they were here. A photograph of a code works from a sofa — together they do not.
